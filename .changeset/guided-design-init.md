New apps get the guided design loop: `@ux-builder/design` as a dev dependency, the `ux-builder-design` skill and an empty design state, with `CLAUDE.md`, `AGENTS.md` and the Home screen pointing to `/ux-builder-design`. `--mcp` also writes `.mcp.json` for the ux-builder MCP server; without it none is written.
