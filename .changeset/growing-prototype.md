One prototype that grows (VIP List session 2, where the step prototype showed "Item 1" and "Field 1" and stopped changing after step 3). Wireframes and step prototypes draw each screen with its own content: list columns and rows, detail fields and form fields from its content labels, with product-neutral sample values (names, phones, dates, times, cities, counts) and form controls by what each label asks for. Once the app is built, `ux-builder-design prototype --step` builds it and the check audits its routes, so from step 6 the prototype the person uses is the one checked. The audit's `serveFolder` serves an app's routes (`spa`).
