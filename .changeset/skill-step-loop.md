The design skill teaches the per-step loop (US-170): draft the rest, draw the step prototype, check it, show it, then ask "continue, revise, or shall I finish the rest?". It sets the appearance from the product, reports status in four plain lines (ready, blocked by, who is handling it, what I need from you) without engineering terms, never ends with the person owning a dependency, offers recommendations as defaults but never groups approvals, and never lets the theme block. The glossary explains "step prototype" and "finish for me".
