`ux-builder-blueprint check` applies an app's experience principles from `.ux-builder/principles.json` as project rules (`no-step-before`, `max-required-inputs`, `max-steps`, `no-modal-in`); each finding is `project.<PR-id>` and names the principle. `principleFindings` is exported from `@ux-builder/ui/blueprint`.
