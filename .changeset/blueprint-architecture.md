Screens may declare `purpose`, `content` (the information on them, highest priority first) and `interactionKinds`. New warnings: `screen.purpose` (once a blueprint models tasks or content) and `consistency.same-kind` (two tasks handle the same kind of interaction with different patterns). Wireframes show each screen's content in its order, and `propose`'s wireframe drafts list its purpose and content.
