What VIP List sessions 3 and 4 needed (US-173). `ux-builder-design step batch` records the person's one answer to steps 4 to 6. From step 5 the step check walks every journey in a browser (`walkJourneys` in `@ux-builder/audit`), and a step's first check reuses an earlier step's audited pages or the same app bundle. A report merges participants with the same problem into one finding. The lint lets a QR code or barcode marked `data-graphic` keep its own `<svg>` and black and white. In the blueprint, `stateNotices` take the `values` a state changes, and content items may be marked `compact` for phone lists (the first three otherwise); an error notice holds back the screen's main action. Drawn prototypes show each screen's other actions, a readable bar in dark, and counts that say what they count. An app built from a design takes the design's appearance and audits every designed screen; its business-rule notes show only from the prototype controls, and a form's main action follows its fields. A journey that ends where it started is no longer scored as "0 moves would do", alternatives are drawn in the design's appearance, and test-kit goals keep their verb.
