Two optional colour roles, `color.destructive` and `color.on-destructive`: the body of a destructive action's button
(a delete confirmation) and the words on it. A brand that sets neither looks as before (they equal
`color.status-negative` and `color.on-accent`). vk-synaptiq sets Lava #DB1F26 with white in both appearances, so its
destructive buttons stay Lava in dark while its red text keeps the lighter, readable tint.
