`ux-builder-blueprint alternative new|choose` makes alternative blueprints (`app.blueprint.<name>.json`) and records the choice and its reason in the design state; `score` gives each journey's predicted effort; `compare` writes one page comparing alternatives for the same tasks, marking the best per task and dimension, with the agent's judgments labelled preliminary. MCP gains `score_blueprint` and `compare_blueprints`.
