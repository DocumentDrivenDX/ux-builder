Optional colour roles `color.selected` (default `color.structure`) and `color.stance-recommended`, `-conditional`,
`-watch` and `-avoid` (defaults: the positive, info, caution and negative statuses), and the component token
`button.height-small` (default 26px). A theme that sets none looks as before. vk-synaptiq takes the Synaptiq Figma
library's decisions: a darkened-Arctic selection (#527693) for checks, radios and switches with a white thumb, 8px
control, notice and menu corners, a 2px invalid-field edge, a brighter dark card outline (#616161), 32px small buttons,
and its stance colours (Agave, darkened Arctic, deepened Stone, Lava).
