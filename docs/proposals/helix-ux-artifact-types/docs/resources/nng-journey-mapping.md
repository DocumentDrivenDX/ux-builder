# NN/g Journey Mapping 101

## Source

- URL: https://www.nngroup.com/articles/journey-mapping-101/
- Owner: Nielsen Norman Group (NN/g), a user-experience research and consulting firm
- Accessed: 2026-10-21

## Summary

A journey map is a visual narrative of the steps one actor takes to reach one
goal. NN/g describes five elements: the actor (one persona per map); the
scenario and the actor's expectations; journey phases (the actor's high-level
stages); the actions, mindsets (thoughts) and emotions in each phase; and the
opportunities the map reveals, with who owns them and how they will be
measured. A map is usually read in three bands: the top names the actor,
scenario and goals; the middle shows the phases with actions, thoughts and
emotions; the bottom lists insights, opportunities and ownership.

## Relevant Findings

- One actor and one scenario per map keeps the narrative coherent.
- Current-state maps describe today's experience; future-state maps describe
  the intended one.
- Maps should rest on research; assumption-based maps are a starting point to
  be validated.
- Opportunities without an owner and a metric rarely lead to change.

## Use in HELIX

Grounds `user-journey-map`: the template's top, middle and bottom zones and its
required fields map one-to-one to the five elements.
