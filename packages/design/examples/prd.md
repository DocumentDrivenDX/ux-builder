# Product Requirements Document — River Path

## Summary

River Path helps anglers find a river to fish and the public access to reach it.

## Users and Scope

Anglers who fish rivers they do not know well.

### User Segments

| User | Context | Needs | Evidence | Confidence | Status |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Weekend anglers new to an area | On a phone at the riverbank, one hand free | Find a river near me, Know where to park legally | anglers | medium | validated |

### User Needs

| Need | Importance | Evidence | Confidence | Status |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Find a river near me | high | anglers | high | validated |
| Know where to park legally | high | anglers | medium | validated |

## Requirements

- FR-1: Show rivers near the person, with their public access points.
