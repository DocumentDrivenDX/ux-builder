# TK-1: Finding a river and a place to reach it

## Scenarios

| Task | Journey | Goal given to the participant |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | J-find | You have a free afternoon. Find a river within an hour where you could fish. |

## Success criteria

| Task | Criteria |
|---|---|
| 1 | US-001-AC1 |

## Facilitator script

1. Thank the participant; ask them to think aloud.
2. Read the task; do not point at the screen.
3. If they are stuck for two minutes, offer one hint and note an assist.

## Consent

The session is noted, not recorded. Notes use a pseudonym. You can stop at any time.

## Screener

- Has visited a river for recreation in the last year.
