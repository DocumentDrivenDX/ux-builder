# Writing a research evidence

One research source (an interview, an analytics export, support tickets, a survey, usability observations or a session transcript): where the owner keeps it, and the masked excerpts the synthesis cites. Raw material is never copied in (ADR-039).

Record where the source is kept, never its raw contents. Quote only the short
excerpts your synthesis relies on, each masked and attributed to a pseudonym
(P1, P2) listed under participants. Separate what people said from what they
did. A synthesis note is your reading of the excerpts, labelled as such.

Use `template.md`; `example.md` shows a finished one. `ux-builder-design validate` checks it.
