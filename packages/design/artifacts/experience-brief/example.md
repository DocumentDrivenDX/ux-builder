# Experience brief: River Path

## Product

- River Path helps people discover rivers and the places to reach them. [confirmed]

## Target users

- Outdoor enthusiasts, anglers and river explorers. [confirmed]
- Most use a phone at the river or the night before. [hypothesis]
  Confirm by: ask in each interview where and when they plan a trip.

## Primary outcome

- People find a suitable river and a public access point quickly. [confirmed]

## Primary jobs

- J-1: Find relevant rivers. [confirmed]
- J-2: Evaluate a river's characteristics. [assumed]
- J-3: Locate access points. [confirmed]

## Experience principles

- Minimal initial configuration. [confirmed]
- No mandatory account creation. [confirmed]
- Location-aware discovery. [assumed]

## Constraints

- Mobile web application; limited development resources. [confirmed]

## Open questions

- Q-1: Search first or map first?
- Q-2: How much personalization is appropriate?
