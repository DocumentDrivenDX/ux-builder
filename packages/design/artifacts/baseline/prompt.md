# Writing a baseline

What an existing app is today, drafted in its first guided session from the blueprint, the HELIX documents and the latest audit and assessment. Every drafted point is current state, assumed, until the person confirms it.

Start from what `ux-builder-design baseline` wrote. Describe what exists, not
what should exist. Every drafted brief point, job and task stays marked
"current state, assumed" until the person confirms it. Then ask the person
what they want to improve.

Use `template.md`; `example.md` shows a finished one. `ux-builder-design validate` checks it.
